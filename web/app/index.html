Data

Hold a button: click and hold, or Alt-click to latch it (click again to release). Keyboard: Shift = Shift, Space = Start/Stop, ←/→ turn the encoder, Enter pushes it. Pots and the encoder turn by dragging up/down or scrolling. Z–M and Q–I play the selected VA slot; [ ] change its octave.

How to use the LXR

Your first beat

  1. Drum 1 is selected after switch-on.
  2. Click step buttons 1, 5, 9, 13. Their LEDs light: a four-on-the-floor kick.
  3. Press Space (or Start). A moving LED shows the playhead.
  4. Click Snare, then steps 5 and 13. Click Hihat cl, then every odd step.
  5. Balance the voices with the faders.

Shaping the sound

  • The display's top row names four parameters, the bottom row shows their values. A > at the end means the page has more parameters.
  • The four pots below the display change those four parameters.
  • The data encoder moves the cursor (the selected parameter is in capitals) or changes its value; push it to switch between the two.
  • In Voice mode, select buttons 1–8 switch between the voice's pages (oscillator, filter, envelopes, modulation, LFO…).
  • A voice button opens that voice for editing.

  • Step edit (press Voice while a voice page is shown): click a step to edit its own settings (velocity, probability, note). The 8 select buttons are its sub-steps, for flams and rolls.
  • Shift + step button selects the function printed under steps 8–16: Kit, Morph, Song, Project, Config, Reload, Last (step, the pattern length), FX, Pattern.
  • Page ▲/▼ switch bars in patterns longer than 16 steps. Hold Copy while pressing Page ▲ to copy the current bar into the next one.

Holding two buttons with a mouse

For Shift, hold the Shift key and click. For any other button, such as Copy, Alt-click it to latch it down, click the second button, then click the latched one to release it.

Saving

The firmware autosaves the kit and pattern 2 seconds after the last edit, but not while the sequencer runs: stop it before closing the tab. Use Save for named projects and Export SD card for a backup.
