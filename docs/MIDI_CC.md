# Which CC moves what

Generated by tools/registry.py from registry/*.json. Do not edit.

Pitch bend and program change reach VA, Dexed, Plaits and Rings too (the end of this page).

A CC on a channel that plays VA, Dexed, Plaits or Rings moves that part's parameter, at the time
the message is stamped with, and the panel follows. A channel that plays the LXR passes its CCs
to the firmware, by the firmware's own map and MIDI settings. The whole range 0-127 covers the
parameter's range, so a parameter with few values takes equal slices of it: `steps` says how many.

CC 21-28 stay with a knob box (knobs 1-8 of the device on show). A preset is not on a CC: a
program change loads it.

## Pitch bend

Bends the part's sounding notes and the next ones by 2 semitones at the wheel's ends. A sequencer can
set another range per channel with RPN 0 (CC 101 = 0, CC 100 = 0, then CC 6 = the semitones, up to 24).
Reset All Controllers (CC 121), a Stop of the port and a change of what the channel plays end a bend.

## Program change

Loads a preset into the part, as its panel does: the panel follows, and the sound set keeps it. A number
the part does not have changes nothing. Program 0 is the panel's #1 (sequencers that count from 1 show
it as 1).

- **VA**: the slot's presets, 0-23.
- **Dexed**: the programs of the part's bank, 0-31 (CC 12 chooses the bank).
